01 / The essentials

Build a study system that works for you.

These methods can make studying more structured and help me move beyond simply rereading information.

METHOD 01

Active Recall

Close the textbook and try to explain a concept from memory. Answer questions without looking at the notes, then check what needs improvement.

Try this: Write five questions after each lecture and answer them the next day.
METHOD 02

Spaced Repetition

Review material over several days instead of trying to memorise everything in one long session.

Try this: Review a topic today, tomorrow, later in the week and again before an assessment.
METHOD 03

Pomodoro Sessions

Work with full attention for a manageable period, then take a short break. Adjust the timing to suit the task and your concentration.

Try this: Start with 25 minutes of focused study followed by a five-minute break.
METHOD 04

Mind Maps

Connect related ideas visually. This can help organise complicated topics and show how concepts relate to one another.

Try this: Put the main topic in the centre and branch out into definitions, examples and applications.
METHOD 05

Practice Questions

Test understanding through exercises, worked examples, quizzes and past assessments when they are available and permitted.

Try this: Keep a list of mistakes and write down how you would solve each question correctly.
METHOD 06

Collaborative Learning

Discuss difficult ideas with classmates, explain concepts to each other and share different approaches to solving problems.

Try this: Give each group member a topic to explain during a revision session.

03 / The bigger picture

My roadmap towards data analytics.

My long-term ambition is to become a data analyst and eventually establish my own data analytics firm. This is a learning roadmap I can work towards progressively, rather than a list of skills I am claiming to have already mastered.

Build the foundations

Strengthen mathematics, statistics, logical reasoning and the ability to interpret information accurately.

Learn spreadsheets

Practise organising data, using formulas, cleaning tables and creating charts with spreadsheet tools.

Explore coding

Progress into Python, data manipulation and introductory database skills such as SQL.

Create a portfolio

Work on small projects using appropriate datasets, explain the findings clearly and keep improving through feedback.

05 / My principles

Learn with integrity.

Academic growth is about more than achieving results. It also means being honest about my work, respecting other people's ideas and taking responsibility for my learning.

01 · Give credit
Acknowledge sources and cite ideas that are not my own.
02 · Be original
Explain concepts in my own words and submit my own work.
03 · Verify information
Check facts and use credible academic sources.
04 · Use tools responsibly
Follow institutional rules when using AI and other tools.
