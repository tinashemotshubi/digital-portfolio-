02

THE PURPOSE

Not every professional message needs to be long.

The email is the introduction. The attachments carry the details.

When applying for an opportunity, the accompanying email should make it easy for the recipient to understand who is writing, why they are writing and what documents have been included.

A GOOD APPLICATION EMAIL
  • Has a clear subject line
  • Uses a professional greeting
  • States the purpose immediately
  • Mentions the attached documents
  • Ends with a polite closing

BEFORE YOU HIT SEND

The small details matter.

01

Check the recipient.

Make sure the email is going to the correct organisation or contact person.

02

Use clear filenames.

Give attachments descriptive names so they are easy to identify and organise.

03

Actually attach the files.

It sounds obvious, but always double-check that the CV and application letter are attached.

04

Read it once more.

Check spelling, grammar, contact details and the subject line before sending.

COMMUNICATION IS PART OF THE SKILL SET

How you communicate is part of your introduction.

A well-written message shows consideration for the person receiving it. Keep it simple, relevant and respectful.
