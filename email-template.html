Digital Communication

Say It with Purpose.

Professional emails are an important part of academic and working life. They help me ask questions, introduce myself, follow up on opportunities and express appreciation clearly and respectfully. This page contains three reusable email examples.

Clear. Courteous. Professional.

Every effective email should have a useful subject line, an appropriate greeting, a clear message and a respectful closing.

My Email Templates

EMAIL 01

Internship Enquiry

Introducing yourself and asking about opportunities.

To: absa@gmail.com
Subject: Enquiry About Technology Internship Opportunities

Dear Hiring Manager,

My name is Tinashe Motshubi, and I am a first-year student pursuing a bachelor's degree in the BIDA programme. I am writing to enquire whether your organisation offers internship opportunities for students interested in the technology sector.

I am particularly interested in developing my knowledge of data analytics and gaining practical exposure to a professional working environment. I am eager to learn, improve my skills and gain insight into how technology can be used to solve real-world problems.

I would appreciate any information you could share about available opportunities, eligibility requirements and application procedures.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Kind regards,

Tinashe Motshubi

02 · Professional Etiquette

Small Details Matter

Good email habits make communication easier for both the sender and the recipient.

Use a Clear Subject

Tell the recipient what the email concerns. Avoid vague subjects such as "Hello" or "Important".

Keep It Focused

State your purpose early, use short paragraphs and include the details needed for the recipient to respond.

Proofread Before Sending

Check spelling, names, attachments, recipient addresses and the tone of your message before pressing send.

03 · A Simple Formula

How to Structure a Professional Email

  1. Subject line: Make the purpose easy to recognise.
  2. Greeting: Use the recipient's name when known.
  3. Introduction: Explain who you are when necessary.
  4. Main message: State your request or information clearly.
  5. Closing: Thank the recipient or explain the next step.
  6. Signature: Include your full name and appropriate contact details.
