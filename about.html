A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

More than a student ID.

I'm Tinashe. A curious mind, an easygoing person, and someone still figuring out exactly how far curiosity can take him.

PERSONALITY

Quirky by nature. Empathetic by choice. Always learning.

02

BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

So, who am I?

I would describe myself as someone who enjoys keeping things simple, but never stops being curious.

I'm naturally easygoing and a little quirky. I enjoy being around people, understanding different perspectives and finding meaning in the little things. Being an empath is a part of how I experience the world; I tend to pay attention to how people feel, not just what they say.

I don't necessarily have every part of my future mapped out, and I'm learning to be comfortable with that. For now, I'm interested in growing, trying things, making mistakes and becoming more intentional about the person I'm developing into.

MY ACADEMIC WORLD

Learning with purpose.

CURRENTLY

Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

I'm a first-year student pursuing a degree in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences.

The programme gives me an opportunity to explore how information, technology and business thinking can come together to solve real problems.

ACADEMIC INTERESTS

Science still has my attention.

Biology and chemistry are subjects I have always found interesting. I enjoy the process of understanding how things work, how different elements connect and why certain things happen.

That curiosity continues to influence how I approach learning, even as I explore a different academic field.

WHERE I'M HEADED

Big plans, one step at a time.

MY CURRENT CAREER GOAL

To become a data analyst.

I'm interested in learning how data can be used to uncover patterns, answer questions and help organisations make better decisions.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Building something of my own.

Beyond becoming a data analyst, one of my long-term ambitions is to establish my own data analytics firm. I would like to build something that creates value, solves problems and gives me the opportunity to contribute meaningfully through what I know.

It's a big ambition, but every journey has to start somewhere. Mine starts with learning.
