TM Reflect · Learn · Grow
A note to myself

Personal growth begins with honest reflection. As a first-year BIDA student, I am still adjusting to the expectations of university life, discovering my strengths and developing habits that will support my future.

I see myself as quirky, easygoing and empathetic. These qualities influence how I interact with people and approach new experiences. At the same time, I understand that having potential is only the beginning. I must put that potential into practice through effort, consistency and a willingness to learn.

Looking inward

Lessons beyond the classroom.

Reflection helps me connect my everyday experiences with the skills, attitudes and habits I want to develop.

01 / SELF-AWARENESS

Understanding who I am.

I recognise that being easygoing and empathetic can help me build positive relationships. I enjoy understanding other people and appreciate the different perspectives they bring to a situation.

I also want to become more intentional about how I use my time, respond to challenges and maintain focus on my academic priorities.

02 / LEARNING

Becoming a better student.

Studying at university requires responsibility, independent learning and consistent effort. I want to make the most of my degree by engaging with course material, asking questions and seeking help when I need clarification.

My interest in biology, chemistry and data analytics reminds me to remain curious and approach unfamiliar concepts with an open mind.

03 / CHALLENGES

Learning to work through difficulty.

Challenges are an opportunity to identify what I do not yet understand and decide how to improve. Rather than expecting everything to be easy, I want to develop patience and persistence.

When a task feels difficult, I can break it into smaller steps, review my approach and seek constructive feedback.

04 / RESPONSIBILITY

Showing up with intention.

I value punctuality, meeting deadlines and prioritising important tasks. These habits can help me become a dependable student and a reliable future colleague.

My goal is to turn these values into consistent actions through planning, preparation and regular review of my responsibilities.

“
I don't have to know everything today. I just have to keep learning and become better than I was yesterday.

A reminder to keep growing

The growth plan

Turning reflection into action.

Reflection is most useful when it leads to practical changes. These are areas I want to keep developing throughout my studies.

01

Strengthen my study routine

I want to organise my study time, revise consistently, keep track of deadlines and avoid leaving important tasks until the last minute.

02

Build confidence through practice

I can improve by participating in class, practising new concepts, working through examples and asking questions when something is unclear.

03

Develop professional and analytical skills

I intend to strengthen my communication, teamwork, problem-solving and technology skills as preparation for a future career in data analytics.

Looking ahead

My future deserves
my effort today.

My long-term ambition is to become a data analyst and establish my own data analytics firm. Reaching that goal will require more than ambition. I will need relevant knowledge, practical experience, integrity, discipline and the ability to work with people.

As I continue my studies, I want to remain open to feedback, acknowledge my mistakes and celebrate genuine progress. I may still be discovering the professional I want to become, but I can take meaningful steps towards that future now.

A reminder for my next reflection What specific experience challenged me this term? What did I personally do, what did I learn, and what evidence shows that I improved?
Final thoughts

Still becoming. Still believing.

This reflection reminds me that growth is a continuing process. I have qualities I can build on, skills I still need to strengthen and ambitions that motivate me to keep moving forward.

I want to approach my education with curiosity, treat people with empathy and take responsibility for my development. I do not need to have every answer immediately. What matters is that I remain willing to learn, apply what I learn and keep working towards the future I envision.
