02

A DIFFERENT WAY TO APPROACH STUDYING

Studying is not just about spending more hours with a book.

It is about making the time you spend learning actually count.

University introduces new ideas, unfamiliar concepts and moments when things simply do not make sense immediately.

The goal is not to understand everything in one sitting. It is to develop a way of learning that makes difficult information easier to approach, remember and apply.

03

TOOLS FOR THE STUDENT TOOLBOX

Strategies worth trying.

Different subjects require different approaches. These strategies can help build a more effective study routine.

01

REMEMBER MORE

Active Recall

Instead of repeatedly reading your notes, close the book and try to explain what you remember. Ask yourself questions and test your understanding without looking at the answers.

Try this: Turn your headings into questions.
02

BUILD CONSISTENCY

Spaced Repetition

Revisit information over several days instead of trying to learn everything the night before an assessment. Short revision sessions can help strengthen long-term memory.

Try this: Review today's notes tomorrow.
03

UNDERSTAND THE PROCESS

Practise, Don't Just Read

For mathematics, statistics and programming, understanding a worked example is only the beginning. Try solving a similar problem independently.

Try this: Solve one question without checking the solution.
04

MAKE IT MAKE SENSE

Explain It Simply

Try explaining a difficult concept using ordinary language. If you cannot explain the idea clearly, identify the part you still do not understand.

Try this: Teach the concept to an imaginary student.
05

MANAGE YOUR TIME

Break It Down

Large assignments can feel intimidating. Divide them into smaller tasks and decide what needs attention first.

Try this: Give each study session one clear goal.
06

PROTECT YOUR ENERGY

Take Meaningful Breaks

Studying for hours without stopping is not always productive. Take short breaks, stretch, drink water and return with a clearer mind.

Try this: Work in focused sessions with planned breaks.

A REMINDER TO MYSELF

Progress is not always loud. Sometimes it is just showing up again.

Not every study session will be productive. Not every topic will make sense immediately. That does not mean progress is not happening. Keep asking questions, keep practising and give yourself room to improve.
