RE: APPLICATION FOR INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in an industrial attachment opportunity within your organisation, particularly in the technology field. I am currently a first-year student pursuing a degree in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences.

My studies have introduced me to the relationship between technology, business and the use of information in decision-making. I am interested in developing a stronger understanding of how these areas are applied in professional environments, and I believe an industrial attachment would provide valuable exposure beyond the classroom.

Although I am at the beginning of my professional journey, I bring a willingness to learn, adaptability and a positive attitude towards responsibility. I work well with others, value punctuality and understand the importance of meeting deadlines. My involvement in student leadership has also helped me appreciate the value of cooperation and accountability.

I would welcome the opportunity to learn from experienced professionals, contribute where possible and develop practical skills that complement my academic studies. I am particularly interested in gaining exposure to technology-related work and understanding the day-to-day processes within a professional environment.

Please find my curriculum vitae attached for your consideration. I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss my application further. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Yours faithfully,

Tinashe Motshubi

Applicant
