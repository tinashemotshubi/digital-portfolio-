RE: APPLICATION FOR A TECHNOLOGY INTERNSHIP

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in an internship opportunity within your organisation's technology sector. I am currently a first-year student pursuing a bachelor's degree in the BIDA programme, and I am eager to develop my knowledge, gain practical experience and learn from professionals working in the field.

I am particularly interested in data analytics and the ways information can be organised, interpreted and used to support informed decisions. My long-term ambition is to become a data analyst and eventually establish my own data analytics firm. I believe that an internship would provide valuable exposure to professional environments and help me understand how classroom learning can be applied to real-world challenges.

I consider teamwork, punctuality, meeting deadlines, adaptability and attention to detail to be important strengths in my approach to work and learning. My experience participating in student leadership during senior school also helped me appreciate responsibility and the importance of contributing to a shared goal.

As a developing student, I am aware that I still have much to learn. I would approach an internship with curiosity, a positive attitude and a willingness to receive feedback, follow instructions and take responsibility for assigned tasks. I would welcome the opportunity to observe experienced professionals, strengthen my practical skills and contribute wherever appropriate.

Thank you for considering my application. I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how my interests, personal qualities and commitment to learning could align with your organisation's needs. I look forward to the possibility of hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,
Tinashe Motshubi
